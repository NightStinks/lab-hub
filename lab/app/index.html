Add a new sensor

  1. Plug it in where it will live. After a few seconds it makes its own Wi-Fi network, LAB Zones Setup (or LAB Presence Setup).
  2. On your phone, join that Wi-Fi. A page opens by itself: choose your home Wi-Fi and type its password.
  3. Wait a minute. The sensor appears here, and Home Assistant shows New device discovered in Settings. Press Add there to get its sensors in Home Assistant.
  4. Press the sensor here to set it up: where it is mounted, your walls and your zones.

Moving a sensor to another home? Just plug it in there: if it cannot find its old Wi-Fi for a minute, its setup Wi-Fi comes back on its own. To wipe it completely, plug it in and unplug it again within 10 seconds, five times in a row.
