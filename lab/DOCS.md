# LAB.

Finds your LAB. sensors on the network and opens their setup pages from the Home Assistant sidebar.
No IP addresses or device names to remember, and the pages work away from home through Home Assistant.

## Adding a new sensor

1. Plug it in where it will live. After a few seconds it makes its own Wi-Fi network, **LAB Zones Setup**.
2. On your phone, join that Wi-Fi. A page opens by itself: choose your home Wi-Fi and type its password.
3. Wait a minute. The sensor appears in **LAB.**, and Home Assistant shows **New device discovered**. Press **Add** there to get its sensors in Home Assistant.
4. Open the sensor from **LAB.** to set it up.

## Moving or resetting a sensor

- **Moving it to another home:** just plug it in there. If it cannot find its old Wi-Fi for a minute, its setup Wi-Fi comes back on its own.
- **Wiping it completely:** plug it in and unplug it again within 10 seconds, five times in a row. Everything is erased, including Wi-Fi.
